### About

I've spent 8+ years shipping software across the stack, from enterprise **Identity & Access Management** to consumer marketplaces and multi-tenant SaaS.
I've delivered client projects for teams in **Australia, Germany, the USA** and several other countries, mostly working remotely from Dhaka across time zones.
By day I work on **Angular, Node.js, AWS and Identity Governance** at Allianz Australia.
After hours I run **[ShareDeal](https://sharedealnow.com)**, a group-buying platform that connects Bangladeshi farmers directly with households.

**Right now**

- 🛒 Scaling ShareDeal: customer app, rider app, and a multi-seller marketplace
- 🛡️ Building [OpenIGA](https://github.com/arafatomer66/openiga), an open-source alternative to commercial identity governance suites
- ✍️ Writing free, practical handbooks for developers (see below)
